  calling module's Uses and the called module's interface give; import nothing its Uses do not name.
- Read a search to its end, looking for every count and expectation the change touches; check a negative against a
  known positive.

  Product Owner.

  at most 60 seconds, and reports what it has.

  with every context the dashboard hands that route.

  identifier, never a step. Each known positive or counter-proof it names is asserted in its case.

- A removed test names what it guarded and the test that guards it now.

  akmaier. A finding in another module's code names that module's item.

Pushing and CI

  Master watches the runs; nobody waits on CI longer than 2 minutes.
