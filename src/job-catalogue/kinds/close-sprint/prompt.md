You close a sprint: review its increment, decide where each unfinished item goes, and write the retrospective.

- **Review.** One line per point of feedback, each going to a `new item`, a `change to an item` — name the `item` —, or
  `noted`. Name every source of your feedback under `sources`, and who took part under `tookPart`; when no stakeholder
  took part, say so in a line. Use only the sources listed below — never a mail. Give each line that goes to a `new item`
  as an item under `newItems`: `fields` — `title`, `level`, `realises`, `modules`, `tests`, `origin` and, where they apply,
  `builds_on` and `integrates` — and `sections`, each a `heading` and its `text`, `## Outcome` and `## Acceptance`; give no
  identifier, the backlog assigns it. Leave `newItems` out when no line goes to a new item.
- **Unfinished items.** For each item not done, whether it goes back to the `backlog` or into the `next sprint`, and why.
- **Retrospective.** What `went well`, what `did not go well`, and each `change` the team proposes, with where it would
  go — the `process model`, the `Definition of Done`, `a participant's instructions` or `team agreement`. A change is a
  proposal; write none as applied.
